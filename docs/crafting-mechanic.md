# Inside the Crate — Crafting System

> **Combine parts, build power.**
> The crafting system turns raw loot into permanent upgrades for your fleet.

---

## What is Crafting?

In Inside the Crate, every robot you deploy extracts resources from alien planets. But raw power comes from what you *build* — not just what you find.

The **Crafting Station** lets you combine rare parts into:
- 🤖 **Robots** — custom-built units with guaranteed stats
- ⚙️ **Equipment** — modules that boost Extraction Rate or reduce Power Draw
- 🏗️ **Base Upgrades** — fleet-wide efficiency improvements

---

## How It Works

### 1. Collect Parts
Parts drop from **Lootboxes** and **Minigame victories**. Each part has a type and rarity — Common, Uncommon, Rare, or Epic — and they stack in your inventory.

### 2. Find a Recipe
The Crafting Station shows every available recipe and exactly what you're missing. No guesswork — you always know the exact ingredients required.

### 3. Pay the Cost
Some recipes require a small **CRATE fee** to start. This represents the energy and resources consumed during manufacturing. The fee is set by the game admin and can evolve as new content is added.

### 4. Wait for Fabrication *(some items)*
Complex items take time to fabricate. Once you start a craft, you can close the game and come back later — the manufacturing continues while you're offline.

When it's ready, a **"Collect"** button appears. Items are never lost — they wait for you.

### 5. Collect & Deploy
The finished item goes straight to your inventory, ready to equip or apply.

## The F2P Path

Crafting is designed as the **primary progression path for free-to-play players**:

1. Play **Minigames** → win rare parts as drops
2. Open **Lootboxes** → collect parts of various rarities
3. Combine parts → craft equipment or robots that rival paid items

A dedicated player who wins minigames daily can accumulate enough parts to craft Rare and Epic-tier items without spending a cent.

---

## An Evolving System

The crafting system is built on a **live database** — new recipes can be added at any time without game updates. This means:

- **Seasonal recipes** for limited-time events
- **Exclusive blueprints** tied to rankings or achievements
- **Higher-tier crafts** as the game grows and more parts enter the economy

Every recipe is a **widget** — the same card component is reused across the game and the admin panel. New recipes simply appear in the station the moment they're published.

---

## Why Crafting Matters for the Economy

When parts are consumed in crafting, they leave the economy permanently. This creates natural **deflation pressure** on common parts and sustains the value of rare drops.

Every item crafted is tracked. In a future update, players will be able to see **global crafting statistics** — total robots built, parts consumed, items created across the entire network. A living record of the economy.

## Get Started

1. Open **Lootboxes** or win **Minigames** to collect parts
2. Visit the **Crafting Station** at `/crafting`
3. Find a recipe you can complete and hit **Craft**

The Void Excavator won't build itself.
